A dedicated study guide and technical reference repository for professionals preparing for the **AVIXA Certified Technology Specialist – Design (CTS-D) Certification Exam**, covering audiovisual system design, project coordination, documentation, and system verification.

---

## Exam Overview

The AVIXA CTS-D certification validates the professional ability to assess client requirements, develop audiovisual system designs, coordinate with other professionals, and support project implementation and closeout.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination administered at a Pearson VUE testing center.
- **Target Audience**: Audiovisual system designers, AV design engineers, and professionals responsible for planning and documenting AV solutions.
- **Prerequisites**: An active CTS certification in good standing is required. AVIXA recommends 3–4 years of audiovisual design experience.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Conduct a Needs Assessment

- Identify client requirements, user needs, and functional expectations.
- Review or develop client technology master plans.
- Generate or review program reports and project requirements.

### 2. Coordinate with Other Professionals

- Coordinate AV design activities with architectural and interior design professionals.
- Collaborate with structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing professionals.
- Coordinate with IT, networking, acoustical, life safety, security, and other project specialists.

### 3. Develop and Document AV Designs

- Create conceptual audiovisual system designs.
- Adapt designs to site conditions and architectural and engineering drawings.
- Produce final design packages, drawings, bills of materials, bid specifications, and scopes of work.

### 4. Deploy AV Designs

- Support project execution and implementation activities.
- Verify audiovisual system performance against project requirements.
- Complete project closeout documentation, including as-built drawings, system manuals, and training information.
